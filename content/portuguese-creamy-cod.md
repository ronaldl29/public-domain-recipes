Called _Bacalhau com Natas_ in Portuguese, this is a traditional Portuguese dish.

- ⏲️ Prep time: 15 min
- 🍳 Cook time: 50 min
- 🍽️ Servings: 2

## Ingredients

- 2 cod fillets (~250 grams)
- 400ml of milk, either whole or semi-skimmed
- half an onion, sliced
- 1/2 tablespoons of flour
- 500 g of potatoes
- 150 ml fresh cream
- grated cheese (mozzarella works fine)
- ground nutmeg

## Directions

1. Pour the milk into a saucepan and bring to boil.
2. Place the cod fillets inside and boil for 10-15 minutes.
3. Slice the onions and place in a frying pan with some olive oil. Fry until golden.
4. Drain the cod (keep the milk!) and shred on a chopping board.
5. Mix with the onions, fry for a couple of minutes, then add the milk you used to boil it with.
6. Add the flour to thicken. Keep on low heat for 5-10 minutes until you get the desired consistency.
7. Chop the potatoes into cubes roughly the size of a dice. Lightly fry them in some oil or olive oil (not deep frying) for around 5-10 minutes, in low heat.
8. Get an oven tray, oil it with some olive oil, then put in the cod and the potatoes, mixing well. Add the ground nutmeg, salt and pepper.
9. Pour the cream on top and sprinkle with cheese.
0. Put it in the oven and bake for about 20 minutes, until the top is golden and crispy.
9. Enjoy!
