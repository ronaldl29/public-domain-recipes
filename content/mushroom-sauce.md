This is a simple mushroom sauce recipe. Goes great with [pasta](/pasta/).

- ⏲️ Prep time: 5 min
- 🍳 Cook time: 30 min
- 🍽️ Servings: 2 (200 g pasta)

## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp finely chopped champignons
- 1 tbsp chopped onion
- 150 g cream or milk
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp wheat flour

## Directions

1. Place the butter in a frying pan and place over medium heat.
2. Add all the chopped onions, mix well and leave for 10 minutes or until the onions turn a little translucent golden.
3. Add finely chopped champignons, mix well and add salt. Close the lid for 5 minutes.
4. Add a tablespoon of flour, stir.
5. After 3 minutes, add cream (or milk), mix well and close the lid for 10 minutes. If the sauce thickens too much, add a tbsp milk.
6. Add salt, pepper and mix.
