Very simple, yet great drink for cold mornings or evenings.

- ⏲️ Prep time: 5 min
- 🍽️ Servings: 1

## Ingredients

- 1 egg
- 1 cup milk

## Directions

1. Separate the yolk from the white.
2. Beat the yolk with 1 tablespoon sugar.
3. Warm the milk.
   It must be hot but not _boiling_ hot.
4. Add warm milk to the beaten yolk and stir fast to make a little foam.
